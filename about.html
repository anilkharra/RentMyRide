Who we are

A Nashik-first travel operator, backed by Eeshan Group.

RentMyRide is a group company of Eeshan Group — Nashik's established transport and logistics operator. We started with a simple observation: most cab bookings stop at the pickup and drop. We plan the entire day around it — food, sightseeing, shopping, and a quiet evening — so your trip feels guided, not just chauffeured.

Every booking is led by an experienced guide-cum-driver who knows the temples, the routes, and the good local stops that don't show up in a search result. Being part of Eeshan Group means a maintained fleet and trained drivers behind every trip we plan.
